# Design System: Obsidian Glow

The visual identity is **Tactile Cyber-Modern / Obsidian Glow**:

- **Canvas:** Deep obsidian `#0b0f19` in dark mode, crisp clean slate `#f8fafc` in light mode.
- **Card Surfaces:** Frosted glassmorphism panels with `backdrop-blur-md`, subtle translucent backgrounds, and crisp hairline micro-borders (`border border-white/10`).
- **Domain Color Coding:**
  - Health & Vitality: Emerald `#10b981`
  - Deep Work & Craft: Cyan `#06b6d4`
  - Mind & Wisdom: Violet `#8b5cf6`
  - Daily Discipline: Amber `#f59e0b`
- **Interactive Feedback:**
  - Active button spring compressions (`active:scale-95`).
  - Luminous progress ring animations with glowing ambient drop-shadow.
  - Confetti / particle micro-bursts on 100% daily target achievement.
  - Floating undo toast with instant reversal.
