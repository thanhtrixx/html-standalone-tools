  - Highlight **Rolling 30-Day Consistency Score**: `94.2%` with a reassuring shield badge.

### Scene 5: Local-First Privacy & Zero-Knowledge Sync (00:42 – 00:52 | Duration: 10s)

  - Animated lock icon pulsing with AES-GCM-256 badge: `🔒 Zero-Knowledge Encrypted`.

- **Visuals**:
  - Mobile mock-up showing iOS **Add to Home Screen** action $\to$ App icon launching fullscreen instantly.
  - Sleek central card:
