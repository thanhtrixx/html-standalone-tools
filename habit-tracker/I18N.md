# Internationalization & Bilingual Lexicon (`I18N.md`)

This document defines the official bilingual terminology mappings between **English (`en`)** and **Vietnamese (`vi`)** for the **Atomic Habit & Routine Tracker** (`habit-tracker`).

All user-facing text in code, tooltips, toasts, modals, and test suites must conform strictly to this dictionary.

---

## 🔤 Formatting & Pluralization Rules

- **Number Formatting**:
  - English: `Intl.NumberFormat('en-US')` (e.g., `2,500 ml`, `85%`)
  - Vietnamese: `Intl.NumberFormat('vi-VN')` (e.g., `2.500 ml`, `85%`)
- **Date Formatting**:
  - English: `Wed, Sep 12` / `September 12, 2026`
  - Vietnamese: `T4, 12 thg 9` / `12 tháng 9, 2026`
