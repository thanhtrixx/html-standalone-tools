# English Shadowing Player — Internationalization (i18n) Dictionary & Copy Standards

## 🌍 Language Strategy & Invariants

- **Supported Locales**: English (`en`) and Vietnamese (`vi`).
- **Default Locale**: English (`en`), with auto-detection from `localStorage` or `navigator.language`.
- **100% Bilingual Parity**: Every user-facing string must exist in both `en` and `vi` tables.
